The HMLR notice's 2021 year is the attribution specified by the publisher.

HMLR permits commercial and noncommercial use under OGL, subject to third-party rights. Its Address Data includes postcodes, as well as address fields. Published permissions cover personal/noncommercial address use and display for residential property price information services; other address uses require permission from the rights holders. Aggregating to a sector does not establish that every proposed commercial use is cleared.

This repository publishes area-level property-price statistics, not individual property records. Advertising and market-planning examples demonstrate analytical possibilities. Before commercial activation, check intended data use against upstream rights and the receiving platform's current rules. The repository does not provide a ready-to-upload advertising audience or legal clearance.

Exact source URLs and vintage limitations are in [the methodology](docs/methodology.md).
